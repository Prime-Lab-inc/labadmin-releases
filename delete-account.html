Delete your account

Prime Lab Admin (Android and iPhone app) · Prime Dental Lab Inc.

1. Permanent deletion

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Upon deletion, your account and all information associated with it are permanently removed and cannot be restored. A new account will not contain any data from a deleted account.

2. Your responsibility before deletion

You are solely responsible for exporting or saving any information you wish to keep before you delete your account. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Prime Dental Lab Inc. shall have no liability for any loss of data, access or information resulting from the deletion of your account.

3. How to delete your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account → confirm.
  • Without the app: open id.primelabinc.com/delete-account, sign in and press Delete account.

Uninstalling the app from your device does not delete your account.

4. Data controlled by the dental laboratory

Cases, files, invoices, payments and messages are created, stored and controlled by the dental laboratory with which you work (the "Laboratory"). Prime Dental Lab Inc. does not host, own or control such data and is not responsible for its retention or deletion. Any request concerning such data must be directed to the Laboratory.

5. Information we may retain

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Prime Dental Lab Inc. may retain certain information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation, tax or accounting obligations, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, including billing records of laboratory subscriptions.
